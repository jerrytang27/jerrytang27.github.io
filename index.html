About Me

Hello! I am Erqiang Tang, a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering (ECE) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I am privileged to work under the supervision of Prof. Khaled B. Letaief.

My research lies at the intersection of generative modeling and wireless communications, with a focus on leveraging modern generative AI techniques for wireless signal processing. I am interested in how data-driven generative priors can improve signal reconstruction, channel estimation, and resource allocation in next-generation wireless systems.

I am always open to academic discussions and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out via email if you are interested in related topics.
